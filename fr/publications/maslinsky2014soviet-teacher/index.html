Советский учитель на фоне школьной повести: корпусная перспектива [Soviet teacher against the background of the school novel: a corpus perspective]

Résumé

This article analyzes the discursive context surrounding the portrayal of teachers in Soviet school-themed fiction of the post-war period. The study draws on a corpus of texts by 37 authors—totaling 1.8 million words—written between 1940 and 1980 and focused on schools and schoolchildren. Using probabilistic topic modeling (LDA), the author automatically analyzed the content of episodes mentioning the terms director (principal), zavuch (head teacher/deputy principal), uchitel (male teacher), and uchitelnitsa (female teacher), identifying topics that appeared in these episodes with significantly higher or lower frequency than in the corpus as a whole. In terms of thematic profiles, the term uchitelnitsa stands in stark contrast to the others. Key contrasts distinguishing the thematic profiles of these terms include the discourse of upbringing versus the routine of classroom instruction, the Komsomol and the Young Pioneers, and themes of emotion and gender.
