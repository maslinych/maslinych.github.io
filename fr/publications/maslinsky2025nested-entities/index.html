Nested entities and quotation attribution in Russian literary prose

Maslinsky, Kirill. 2025. « Nested entities and quotation attribution in Russian literary prose ». Version 1. Репозиторий открытых данных по русской литературе и фольклору. https://doi.org/10.31860/openlit-2024.12-A003.
DOI cls cta

Résumé

A 1M words corpus of Russian literary prose annotated for nested entities (persons, facilities, locations, geo-political entities, vehicles, organizations) and for quotations attributed to speakers. The corpus is comprised of 506 2000-word excerpts drawn from each of the works included in the Corpus of the Narrative Prose of the 19th century (Sobchuk, Lekarevich 2020). The data is suitable for training and evaluation of machine learning models aimed to work with Russian literary prose. This dataset is a part of the wider effort to develop a multilingual version of the BookNLP toolset with Russian language support.
