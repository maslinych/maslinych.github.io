Ageing institutions ossify a cultural field: Evidence from Soviet book publishing for children

Maslinsky, Kirill. 2026. « Ageing institutions ossify a cultural field: Evidence from Soviet book publishing for children ». Manuscrit. octobre.
Submitted, under review
PDF (preprint) cls childlit

Préprint.

Résumé

Some cultural fields undergo an ossification process, with an ever larger share of the produced material featuring already established authors. I argue that this can be explained by the ageing of the institutions of cultural production, without assuming that anyone changed their behavior. The core mechanism is the growing backlog: the probability that a producing unit fills a slot with an item from outside of its own catalog diminishes with the size of that catalog, so a field that stops admitting new producing units will ossify. I test this mechanism on the case of late-Soviet book publishing for children, using digitized bibliographic data covering 1918-1984. Fitting a model in which a board fills a slot with a newcomer at a probability that falls with the size of its accumulated catalog, I find no evidence that the propensity of the boards to publish new authors declined over 1950-1984. Counterfactual tests show that the exposure of an editorial board to its own growing catalog is both necessary and sufficient to explain the diminishing rate of entry of new authors.
