Kirill Maslinsky

I am a quantitative researcher in the humanities working in France, currently a research engineer at INALCO, Paris. These pages provide information about my scholarly interests and publications. My profile is cross-disciplinary, with an educational background in linguistics and literary studies, a PhD in cultural anthropology, and current work focused on computational literary studies, including a quantitative history of Soviet children’s book publishing.

I am strongly committed to open data and open research practices, and see my broader mission as building digital research infrastructure and workflows.
