ThoTDB — A database of the tonal languages of the world 2023–

Research engineerINALCO — ERC project “Theory of Tone”thot.huma-num.fr/db

A linguistic typological database, part of the ERC advanced project “Theory of Tone”. I took part in the database design — including the theoretical and sampling decisions — wrote all backend and frontend code, and deployed the service.

Detcorpus — A corpus of Russian prose for children 2018–

Founder and maintainerdetcorpus.ru

A searchable online corpus (NoSketchEngine-based) of 4000+ fictional and non-fictional works. It originated as a dataset for my own research and has grown into a general-purpose collection and an online service used by researchers, librarians and the wider public.
