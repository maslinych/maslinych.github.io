Teaching

This page lists the university-level courses I taught at various institutions. Teaching materials (syllabi, slides and scripts) are linked where available.

European University at Saint Petersburg

Квантитативный анализ текста — Quantitative text analysis

2022 · MA · Contemporary Political Theory: Language, Knowledge, Power, Subjectivity · Russian

The course offers the basic methodology for using textual collections for quantitative research in humanities and social sciences. The topics covered include the main results in NLP, including stylometry and distributional semantics, as well as application of statistical methods to textual data. Practical exercises use the R programming environment.

Text mining and natural language processing

2021 · MA · Data Analytics for Politics and Society · English · with Sergey Pashakhin

The goal of the course is to provide basic understanding of how to properly use collections of texts as quantitative evidence, and to make this knowledge practical. Scripts in Python.

Computational methods for text analysis

2017–2021 · BA · Sociology and social informatics · English

Computational methods for text analysis promise to aid at a scale where traditional content analysis is not feasible. The course offers the methodological basis required to analyze real textual collections and to replicate methods from recent social research employing computational text analysis. The R programming environment is used as a toolbox.

Искусственный интеллект и большие данные — Artificial intelligence and big data

2018–2020 · BA · Graphic design · Russian

The course introduces students to how artificial intelligence was conceptualized since the inception of the term, and to the core ideas of machine learning. Topics include logic and the theory of algorithms, heuristic search, neural network architectures, multidimensional representations for pattern recognition, and Bayesian inference.

Research seminar “Digital anthropology”

2018–2019 · BA · Sociology and social informatics · English

The seminar is intended for students interested in the qualitative study of social practices in various digital settings, with anthropology as the main disciplinary frame of reference. Seminal works in digital anthropology are read and discussed to understand the field and to “learn by example”, and classroom time and home assignments are devoted to training in designing and writing up a research project, with a particular focus on fieldwork in digital environments. Students also carry out group research projects on a topic of their choice, studying human behavior in some digital environment, discussed in class at every stage.

  • Syllabus (2019)

Цифровые методы для гуманитариев — Digital methods for the humanities

2019 · BA · Philology · Russian · with Alexei Kouprianov

The course introduces students of philology to the basic concepts and methods of data analysis and visualization, and provides practical skills with the software required for it, using the R statistical environment. Topics covered include the foundations of statistics, data visualization, basic quantitative text analysis, and the basics of programming in R. The course gives students the core categories and tools needed for quantitative data analysis in the humanities, and lays the ground for further study of modern methods of data analysis and visualization.

Введение в ОС Линукс — Introduction to the Linux operating system

2018–2019 · MA · Information Systems and Human-Computer Interaction · Russian

Linux and its command line serve as the entry point to the architecture of information systems. This short intro covers the shell as a control interface, the filesystem and access permissions, processes and inter-process communication, pipelines and text-processing utilities as an environment for working with data, concluding with system services and scheduling. Students complete a project building a system that regularly collects weather data from an open data source, aggregates it, and publishes an HTML alert report through a web service.

Компьютерные методы анализа текста — Computational methods for text analysis

2013–2015 · BA · Sociology · Russian

The course introduces sociology students to the landscape of natural language processing and computational linguistics — what's solved, what isn't, and the software available for tackling real text-analysis tasks. It covers the core concepts needed to read further in the field, along with the tools available for working with Russian-language texts.

IPSA Summer School on Methods in Political Science

Text Mining for Political & Social Science

2018 · MA, PhD · English

For social and political sciences, written texts provide essential data for studying ideology and political discourse, conflict, sentiment and political affiliation, among many other things. The workshop covers two levels: an introductory to intermediate part on how words can be used as units of operationalization and analysis, and an intermediate to advanced part on modelling text semantics via spatial metaphor and modelling change of meaning in time.

Illinois Wesleyan University

Anthropology of Childhood

2016 · BA · Liberal arts · English

The ultimate goal of this course is to help students understand childhood in their own culture as a cultural construction by analysing different historical, ethnic and social varieties of childhood experience. By giving an anthropological perspective on the topics in child development, childrearing practices, family life, schooling, work and play, we will seek to make explicit some of those cultural assumptions, values and expectations that together constitute the notion of childhood.
